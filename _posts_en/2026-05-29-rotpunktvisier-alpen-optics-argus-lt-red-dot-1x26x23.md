## ALPEN OPTICS Argus LT Red Dot 1x26x23

![Alpen Optics Argus LT 1x26x23](https://www.geartester.de/uploads/url?path=%2Fgeartester%2Farticles%2FBF0FA4FE_BA22_4C8B_AFE3_20717869B5C7_1_102_o_0e436c.jpeg&width=1920)

### First impression and build quality

The ALPEN OPTICS Argus LT 1x26x23 makes a premium impression from the first moment. The open-emitter design in T6 6061 aluminum feels rugged and well thought out, sits solidly in the hand, and gives off the sense of a product you can trust even under demanding conditions. No sharp edges, no wobbly parts, everything precisely made.

At just 70 grams, the sight is remarkably light, especially noticeable on a handgun, where it barely affects handling. The housing is IPX7 rated waterproof for 30 minutes at one meter depth and nitrogen-purged to prevent internal fogging. What's included stands out too: high and low Picatinny mounts, tools, a protective cap, and a battery are all in the box, not something you can take for granted at this price point.

### Reticle and controls

The Argus LT offers a choice of three reticle patterns: a classic 3 MOA dot for precise aiming, a 32 MOA circle for fast target acquisition at short range, and a combination of both. The 10 brightness levels cover everything from daylight to night-vision compatible, and are easy to cycle through intuitively.

The multi-coating on the large 26x23mm lens delivers a clear, distortion-free image. I didn't notice any reflections or ghosting during testing.

![Reticle and lens](https://www.geartester.de/uploads/url?path=%2Fgeartester%2Farticles%2F21057F51_929F_4E0D_AD02_0EB2FE542377_1_102_o_2d9568.jpeg&width=1920)

### DynamicDot technology and power supply

The absolute standout feature of the Argus LT is its DynamicDot technology. Thanks to a built-in motion sensor, the sight switches on automatically as soon as the gun moves and turns off again after two minutes of standing still. After 24 hours without movement, it powers down completely. In everyday use, that's a genuine advantage: no more manually turning it on and off, the sight is always ready when you need it. This saves the battery significantly, the stated runtime of up to 50,000 hours is of course a theoretical figure, but in testing, power draw was noticeably lower than on sights without this feature.

### Mounting and compatibility

The Argus LT uses the widely adopted RMR footprint, ensuring broad compatibility. Mounting it on my S&W's Picatinny rail using the low mount was straightforward and quick. Once mounted, the sight sits firm and secure, no shifting, no wobble. Rounding out the package is Alpen Optics' lifetime Deluxe warranty.

![Mounted on the handgun](https://www.geartester.de/uploads/url?path=%2Fgeartester%2Farticles%2Fdc1716b1_5f08_4477_9095_1682b3629d18_66343b.jpg&width=1920)

### Adjustment and zeroing

Adjustment happens in 0.3 MRAD increments, equivalent to 3 cm at 100 m per click. Both elevation and windage offer up to 98 clicks of total travel, giving enough range for different distances and mounting setups. The clicks engage cleanly and are easy to feel, making reliable adjustment straightforward.

### Weaknesses

At this point, there's little to criticize. The sight impressed across every area tested. The one caveat: long-term testing over several months is still pending. How the Argus LT holds up under sustained use and after many rounds fired can't be conclusively judged at this stage.

### Conclusion

The ALPEN OPTICS Argus LT Red Dot 1x26x23 is a thoroughly accomplished open-emitter red dot sight that impressed across every area tested. The combination of rugged build quality, the practical DynamicDot technology, a large lens, and three flexible reticle options makes it a very attractive offering. At a price of 249 euros, the sight is also very well positioned and represents a genuine value-for-money argument in its segment. Anyone looking for a reliable, lightweight red dot with an RMR footprint for a handgun will find a strong candidate in the Argus LT.

A clear recommendation, with the caveat that the long-term test is still pending.
