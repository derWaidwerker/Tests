There's no shortage of Kydex holsters out there, but few combine individuality, build quality, and genuine practical fitness for purpose as convincingly as Hexagon Holster out of Styria, Austria. I wore the Glock Pancake Cut holster not just at the range, but mainly out hunting, testing it on patrol walks, while stalking, and in difficult terrain.

### Why a holster makes sense

Anyone carrying a handgun while hunting shouldn't just let it float loose in a jacket pocket. That's uncomfortable, unsafe, and anything but practical in a critical moment. A good holster keeps the gun sitting securely, staying clean, and being instantly available when needed. It's the difference between "having it on you" and actually "being ready to use it," and that's exactly what matters in everyday hunting.

### Testing the Hexagon Holster

From the first impression, it's clear you're not holding a mass-produced product, but a high quality, handmade holster developed with a lot of experience and know-how.

The price sits slightly above the market average, but the build quality and the high degree of customization quickly justify the premium. Behind Hexagon Holster is Marco, who channels his experience from the military, police work, and competitive shooting directly into the products. That background shows in plenty of details: the fit is precise, the material rugged, and the entire construction is clearly built around real-world use.

The configurator is especially impressive, theoretically allowing over 7.5 million different combinations. From colors to gun models to belt loops, almost everything can be tailored to your own needs. The four screw holes on each side are particularly handy, letting you adjust carry height individually and wear the holster with a slight cant. Draw resistance can also be adjusted yourself via a screw. And if a configuration isn't directly selectable, Marco will personally make it to order as a custom "top priority" job, straightforward and personal.

In terms of build quality, the holster plays in the top league. Edges are cleanly finished, the material feels premium, and the whole construction feels sturdy and thoroughly fit for daily use. The holster is closed at the bottom, reliably protecting the gun from dirt, mud, or snow, a point that quickly proves valuable in everyday hunting use. Red dot sights or raised sights are no problem either, the holster is fully optic-ready.

Out in the field, the Pancake Cut holster showed exactly what it was built for. It sits close to the body, carries comfortably, and stays firmly in place through every twist and turn, climbing over obstacles, or working through dense brush. Drawing and holstering happens easily, yet still under control. Reholstering gives a clear "click" confirming the gun has locked in securely. Longer stretches on foot never became uncomfortable, and even sitting, it was comfortable enough that I nearly forgot the gun was there. What stood out especially was how clean and protected the gun stayed, the closed bottom reliably keeps dirt and similar debris from working its way into the holster or the barrel.

A genuine plus is the direct contact with the manufacturer. The Hexagon team makes every holster from the configurator to order, and Marco is happy to hear out any kind of special request. Whether it's a rare firearm, a special light module, or a completely custom Kydex project, Marco is the right person to talk to. He thinks along with you, asks questions, and finds pragmatic solutions. You don't just get a product, you get a tool tailored to your own use.

### Conclusion

The Hexagon Pancake Cut holster won me over completely in hunting use. It combines high quality craftsmanship, an enormous range of configuration options, a perfect fit, and genuine practical performance. The price sits somewhat above standard options, but in return you get a holster that doesn't just tag along, it works reliably, whether stalking, on a patrol walk, or in dense terrain. For anyone who wants to carry their handgun seriously and safely, a Hexagon holster is a clear recommendation.
